# Credits and licenses

Many thanks to everyone who makes their work freely available. The same information is in the app under Settings → “Licenses & Credits”.

## Tile sets {#tile-sets}

### Traditional

- **Images:** dots, bamboo, characters, winds, red and green dragon
- **Author:** FluffyStuff
- **Source:** [riichi-mahjong-tiles](https://github.com/FluffyStuff/riichi-mahjong-tiles)
- **License:** [CC0 1.0 (public domain)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Changes:** editor metadata removed.

Flowers, seasons and the white dragon are original drawings for this app. Which image goes on which tile follows the Unicode Mahjong tiles on Wikimedia Commons (Shizhao); nothing was taken from them. The characters on the flowers and seasons are drawn with the iOS system font PingFang SC.

### Birds

- **Images:** 42 birds
- **Author:** Pedro Machado ([Cerrado Birds](https://web.archive.org/web/20250319095247/https://pedropsmachado.com/cerrado-birds-1)); compiled as a tile set by ffalt
- **Source:** [ffalt/mah](https://github.com/ffalt/mah)
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Changes:** split into individual images, otherwise unchanged.

### Space

- **Images:** 42 space images: planets, moons, constellations, spaceflight, friendly aliens
- **Author:** Good Stuff No Nonsense ([Space Icons](https://goodstuffnononsense.com/hand-drawn-icons/space-icons/)); compiled as a tile set by ffalt
- **Source:** [ffalt/mah](https://github.com/ffalt/mah)
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as stated on the download page. The pack comes with its own license: use in apps is allowed, attribution is appreciated.
- **Changes:** split into individual images. Ten images that depicted or resembled characters and vehicles from films, series and games have been replaced by other images from the same pack: space shuttle, landing capsule, radio telescope, observatory, lunar rover, flag on the moon, space capsule, star, cockpit view, stars. The images themselves are unchanged.

### Zodiac

Original drawings for this app: the twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac as ink drawings and as seals, plus ten objects from moon to pine. Flowers and seasons as in the “Traditional” set.

### Animals

- **Images:** 29 animal heads in the “Round (outline)” style
- **Author:** Kenney Vleugels ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl))
- **Source:** [Animal Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/animal-pack-remastered)
- **License:** [CC0 1.0 (public domain)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Changes:** split from the vector file into individual images, otherwise unchanged.

Paw, bone, fish, carrot, apple, flowers and seasons are original drawings in the same style.

### Mix

The Mix has no images of its own. It shows images from Birds, Space, Zodiac and Animals; their credits apply.

## Sounds {#sounds}

All game sounds – match tones, the win chime, the miss sound, the clacking of tiles, the tone for a full tray and the chimes at the Gate of Masters and at the end of the Master Trials – were created for this app. No third-party sound sources are used.

## Design {#design}

Background, tile bodies and animations are original code and are drawn at runtime.
