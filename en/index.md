# {{ site.app_name }}

A calm Mahjong game to unwind with. No ads, no subscription, no tracking – and no internet needed.

Four modes: Classic, Classic with Tray, Triple Match and Shisen-Sho. Each has its own path: 60 levels up to the Gate of Masters, and 20 Master Trials beyond it. If you just want to play on the side, pick the Daily Challenge or Free Play – every game is new, always solvable, with no stars and no best time.

Designed in the style of Japanese ink painting, with calm music and soft sounds. Choose the tiles you like: traditional or something different – the Zodiac in ink, Birds, Animals or a colorful mix of everything. Light and dark, German and English.

## Status

**In development, testing via TestFlight.**

The plan: free to download, try everything for 14 days, then a one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads.

The app is made by one person in their spare time. Bug fixes and updates for new iOS versions will come, but not on a fixed schedule.
