# Support

Questions, bugs or wishes? Send an email to **[{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }})**.

The app is made by one person in their spare time. Every message is read, but a reply may take a few days.

## Reporting a bug {#bug}

To help find the problem, please include:

- your **iPhone model** (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro),
- the **iOS version** (Settings → General → About),
- the **app version** – you find it at the very bottom of the app's Settings, such as “Mahjong 0.8.1 (Build 9)”,
- and briefly, **what happened**: what did you do, what did you expect, and what happened instead? The mode and level help, and so does a screenshot.

## For TestFlight testers {#testflight}

If you are testing the app through TestFlight, please use **“Send Beta Feedback”** in TestFlight instead: share a screenshot from the app directly with TestFlight, or open the app in TestFlight and tap “Send Beta Feedback”. That way your device and version are included automatically.

It's worth checking the [FAQ]({{ '/en/faq/' | relative_url }}) first – some things are already answered there.
