## The home screen {#start}

<!-- Image: home screen, light -->

At the top right there are three buttons: music on or off, Statistics and Settings. Below them is the main button. It reads “Play” or, while a game is in progress, “Continue”, and takes you to the game you played last.

Below that are two groups:

- **The Path to Mastery:** one tile per mode. It shows how far you have come: levels solved, stars and, below that, your stage, such as “56 levels to the Gate of Masters”, “Master Trials: 7 of 20” or “You are a Master”. Tap it to open the path of that mode.
- **On the Side:** the tiles “Daily Challenge” and “Free Play”.

## The modes {#modes}

Every game is solvable. The board always fits entirely on the screen, with no zooming and no panning. At the start it builds up tile by tile; tap to skip.

**Free or blocked.** Only free tiles can be played. Tiles that are not free yet appear slightly darker (you can turn this off in Settings). When a tile is free depends on the mode:

| Mode | A tile is free when … |
|---|---|
| Classic, Classic with Tray | no other tile lies on it, not even partly, **and** there is room to its left or right |
| Triple Match | no other tile lies on it. The neighbors to the left and right don't matter. |
| Shisen-Sho | always – all tiles lie face up in a single layer. What counts is the connection (see below). |

**A pair doesn't match?** If you tap two tiles that don't belong together, the tile wobbles briefly and a quiet sound plays. The tile you tapped last is then selected, so you can carry on right away.

**Flowers and seasons.** In Classic, Classic with Tray and Shisen-Sho, any flower matches any flower and any season matches any season. In the picture sets these tiles carry a small red mark at the top left: a blossom for the flowers, a sun for the seasons.

### Classic {#classic}

<!-- Image: Classic board -->

Tap two matching free tiles to remove them. The board is cleared once no tile is left. If there is no free pair left, the app offers Shuffle, Undo or Restart.

### Classic with Tray {#tray}

<!-- Image: Classic with Tray board -->

It plays like Classic, with a small tray below the board. You can park a free tile there to reach the tiles beneath or beside it.

- **Parking:** swipe a free tile down, or tap the tile first and then the tray. The next free slot shows an arrow.
- **A parked tile** pairs with a free tile on the board or with another tile in the tray. It cannot return to the board, except with Undo.
- **Slots:** the number of tray slots shrinks along the path: levels 1–20 have three slots, levels 21–40 two, levels 41–60 one. The Master Trials have one slot.
- Some levels can only be solved by parking: there, two matching tiles lie on top of each other.
- The board is cleared once board and tray are empty.

### Triple Match {#triple}

<!-- Image: Triple Match board -->

Every free tile you tap moves into the tray. Matching tiles line up next to each other there, and three matching tiles disappear.

- Any tile with no other tile on top of it is free.
- The tray has seven slots; from level 40 and in the Master Trials it has six.
- If the tray fills up without forming a group of three, the game is lost. Undo helps if you have one left, or simply try again.
- From level 30, some tiles lie face down and only show their image once they are free.
- The board is cleared once board and tray are empty.

### Shisen-Sho {#shisen}

<!-- Image: Shisen-Sho board with connecting line -->

All tiles lie face up in a single layer. Two matching tiles disappear if a line can be drawn between them that bends at most twice and only crosses empty cells. The line may also run around the outside of the board.

- **Tap or drag:** tap the two tiles one after the other, or put your finger on the first tile and drag to the second. The ink trail while dragging only shows where your finger went, not the valid connection. If you let go over an empty spot, nothing happens.
- At the very left edge of the screen, the gesture belongs to the iOS back swipe.
- If the images match but there is no connection, it counts as a miss.
- In Free Play, Shisen-Sho is also available [with gravity](#gravity).

## The Path to Mastery {#path}

<!-- Image: the path -->

**Levels.** Each mode has 60 levels that unlock one after another. The path shows them as stations along a trail through changing landscapes: Morning, Day, Evening, Night, Cherry Blossom and Snow. The next station pulses gently. The levels get harder slowly: more tiles, more layers, more images and fewer assists.

**Stars.** Every level awards up to three stars. You start with three. You lose one if you used an assist, and one more each if you take longer than the target time or longer than twice the target time. The target time grows with the number of tiles. You always keep at least one star. You can replay any level you have played; your best result counts.

**Gate of Masters.** From level 50, the Gate of Masters shimmers at the end of the path. After level 60 it opens. The next time you visit the path, you see it open; a tap ends the moment.

**Master Trials.** Behind the gate, 20 Master Trials await: large, hard boards with just one hint and one undo, and no shuffle. Instead of stars, your best time counts. They also unlock one after another.

**The medal.** Pass all 20 trials of a mode and you are a Master. The medal appears at the end of the path with a scroll, and the tile on the home screen shows “You are a Master” with the badge. The path ends there, the game does not: you can beat your best times at any time, and the Daily Challenge and every level remain open to you.

**Reset progress.** Under Settings → Progress you can start a mode over. Levels, stars and Master Trials then start again, with new games. A path game in progress in that mode ends. Best times, statistics, daily challenges and Free Play are kept.

## On the Side {#on-the-side}

### Daily Challenge {#daily-challenge}

<!-- Image: Daily Challenge with calendar -->

Every day, a new level of medium difficulty awaits in each mode. The calendar shows the days on which you solved it; a ring marks days you started but did not finish. Statistics counts your day streak.

**Continuing the next day.** You can finish a daily challenge you started on its own day and on the following day. Below today's entry it then says “Continue the game from …”. If you win it, it counts for its own day. From the day after that, it lapses without counting, and its day stays marked as started in the calendar. If you start today's challenge of the same mode on the following day, it replaces the old one, also without counting.

### Free Play {#free-play}

<!-- Image: Free Play selection -->

In Free Play you choose a mode and a difficulty and get a new, solvable game every time. No path, no stars, no best time – just play. Your path, stars and best times are not affected.

**Difficulty.** Easy, Medium and Hard correspond to levels of the path, with their board size, tray and assists:

| Mode | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | 40 tiles | 84 tiles | 144 tiles |
| Classic with Tray | 40 tiles, 3 tray slots | 84 tiles, 2 slots | 144 tiles, 1 slot |
| Triple Match | 42 tiles, 7 slots | 90 tiles, with face-down tiles | 141 tiles, 6 slots |
| Shisen-Sho | 30 tiles | 60 tiles | 88 tiles |

**Restart and New Game.** “Restart” plays the same game again. “New Game” deals a different one of the same difficulty – with the “+” button at the top of the game (it asks first, because the game in progress is replaced), on the win card or on the selection screen. If a Free Play game is already in progress in the chosen mode, the selection offers “Continue” and “New Game”.

**Winning.** Instead of stars, the win card shows a golden seal, along with your time and the assists you used.

#### Gravity {#gravity}

Only in Shisen-Sho in Free Play: when you choose Shisen-Sho, a “Gravity” row with a switch appears below “Difficulty”. When it is on, after each pair the tiles above slide down in the columns of the two removed tiles. That changes what can be connected: sometimes it opens paths, sometimes it blocks them. Every game is solvable with gravity too.

- The switch applies to the next new game; a game in progress keeps its rule.
- Undo also brings back the tiles that slid down.
- The path, the Master Trials and the Daily Challenge are always played without gravity.

## Games in progress {#games}

Each mode has three places for games in progress: one for the path (levels and Master Trials share it), one for the Daily Challenge and one for Free Play. Games are saved as you play and are still there after you close the app.

- A new level replaces the level in progress of the same mode without asking. The Daily Challenge, Free Play and the other modes stay as they are.
- “Continue” on the home screen always names only the game you played last. You find the others on their path, in the Daily Challenge (marked “Started” with the play time) or in the Free Play selection.

## Assists {#assists}

The assists sit below the board. Each can be used only a limited number of times per level; the number is shown on the button. Early on the path there are more of them, towards the end fewer.

- **Undo** takes back your last move – in Classic with Tray also a parked tile, in Shisen-Sho with gravity also the tiles that slid down.
- **Hint** shows a possible move: a matching pair, in Triple Match a sensible tile, in Classic with Tray sometimes a tile you should park.
- **Shuffle** rearranges the remaining tiles; the game stays solvable. In Classic with Tray, parked tiles stay where they are. Triple Match and the Master Trials have no shuffle.

Every assist you use costs a star. In the Master Trials you get one hint and one undo.

## Tile sets {#tile-sets}

<!-- Image: Settings, tile set selection -->

Under Settings → Tile Set you choose a set for each mode. The game stays the same, only the images change; a game in progress shows the new set right away. With “All Tiles” you can look at every tile of a set at a large size and choose the set from there.

Each set has 42 images, and each one can be told apart from the others even on small tiles and without color vision. The tiles stay light in both appearances.

| Set | Character |
|---|---|
| **Traditional** | The classic Mahjong tiles: dots, bamboo, characters, winds, dragons, flowers and seasons. The default in Classic, Classic with Tray and Shisen-Sho. |
| **Birds** | 42 colorful birds of the Cerrado, the savanna of Brazil. The default in Triple Match. |
| **Space** | |
| **Zodiac** | The twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac, each one twice: as a dark ink drawing and in light color inside a red seal. Plus ten objects from the same world, from the moon to the pine. |
| **Animals** | Playful on purpose: round animal heads from sloth to walrus, plus paw, bone, fish, carrot and apple. |
| **Mix** | Mixes the images of Birds, Space, Zodiac and Animals on one board. |

<!-- Tile overviews per set to follow -->

**Zodiac: ink and seal are different images.** The ink rat only matches the ink rat, the seal only the seal.

**Mix.** Matching tiles always look the same. Which images come from which set changes from game to game and stays the same on “Restart”. “Traditional” is not part of it.

## Settings {#settings}

Open Settings with the gear at the top right of the home screen.

- **How It Works:** the app's short guide, with pictures for each mode, the path, the Daily Challenge, Free Play and the assists.
- **Appearance:** Automatic (like the iPhone), Light or Dark.
- **Tile Set:** for each mode, with a preview and “All Tiles”.
- **Sound:** Background Music and Game Sounds, each on or off and with its own volume.
- **Display:** “Dim Blocked Tiles” and “Build Animation” (the board builds up tile by tile at the start).
- **Progress:** [reset](#path) a mode, after confirming.
- **Licenses & Credits:** where the tiles, music and sounds come from (as on the [Credits]({{ '/en/credits/' | relative_url }}) page).
- At the very bottom you find the app version, such as “Mahjong 0.8.0 (Build 8)”.

## Statistics {#statistics}

<!-- Image: statistics -->

Open Statistics with the button at the top right of the home screen. For each mode it shows:

- **Levels Solved** and **Stars** on the path, plus how many levels you solved with one, two or three stars.
- **Best Times** per level.
- **Master Trials:** trials passed and best times.
- **Daily Challenges:** those completed in recent days, your day streak and the total.
- **Games:** won, started and play time.
- **Free Play:** won and started per mode and difficulty. Free Play is counted separately from the path.

## Music and sounds {#sound}

While the app is open, calm music plays in changing order. The button at the top of the home screen turns it on or off.

- **Other audio apps:** if a music or podcast app is playing, the game's music stays silent. The game sounds can still be heard. The iPhone's silent switch mutes everything.
- **Match tones:** soft, bell-like tones when you remove tiles; with quick matches they rise. While the music plays they are short, so they don't cover the music; without music they ring out longer. This follows your settings (music on and volume above zero), not whether something is audible at the moment.
- Quiet wooden sounds when tiles move or a pair does not match. Taps themselves are haptic only.

## Accessibility {#accessibility}

- **VoiceOver:** every tile names its image and whether it is free or blocked, in modes with layers also its layer, in Shisen-Sho its row and column. With VoiceOver you play by tapping; dragging is not needed. A selection is shown not only by color but also by an outline and a check mark.
- **Larger Text:** the app follows the iOS text size up to the largest accessibility sizes. Some screens then stack content instead of placing it side by side.
- **Reduce Motion:** if Reduce Motion is on in iOS (Accessibility → Motion), all of the game's movements become cross-fades: building the board, shuffling, undo, the Gate of Masters and the medal (then without sparks). The floating tiles on the home screen stand still.
- **Light and dark:** the app follows the iPhone's appearance or stays light or dark (Settings → Appearance). The tiles always stay light so the images are easy to see.
