# Privacy

In short: **the app collects no data.** In detail:

## The app {#app}

- The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.
- It uses no network: no internet connection, no servers, no accounts.
- No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no third-party components (SDKs).
- Your progress and settings are stored only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, they are gone.

## This website {#website}

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, such as your IP address, the date and time and the page requested, in order to deliver the page and protect the service. Details are in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The site itself sets no cookies, loads no fonts, scripts or content from other servers and uses no statistics or analytics services.

## Contact by email {#email}

If you write to me, I use your email address and the content of your message only to reply to you. I do not pass them on. I delete the message once the matter is settled and no retention obligation applies.

## TestFlight and the App Store {#apple}

The app is distributed through Apple's TestFlight and App Store. Whatever Apple processes in doing so (for example when you download or buy the app) is governed by Apple's terms and [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

I receive crash and usage data via Apple only if you have agreed to share it in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). This data is not linked to your name. If you test via TestFlight, I also see what you send yourself via “Send Beta Feedback”. If I invite you to the test by email, I know your name and email address.

Just send me an email. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
