# Frequently asked questions

### Will my progress survive an update? {#progress}

From version 1.0, yes. Until then the app is in testing (including TestFlight): if the format of the saved progress changes, an update may reset it. The app then simply starts fresh; it does not crash.

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, it is gone.

### Why is the music silent? {#music}

If another app is playing audio, such as music or a podcast, the game's music stays silent. The game sounds can still be heard. If the iPhone's silent switch is on, everything is muted. You can also turn music and game sounds on and off separately in Settings under “Sound”.

### How do I change the language? {#language}

The app has no language setting of its own. It follows your iPhone's language. To change it for this app only, go to the iOS Settings → Apps → {{ site.app_short_name }} → Language. German and English are available.

### Which iPhones does the app run on? {#devices}

On iPhones with iOS 26 or later, in portrait only. It is tested on an iPhone 15 Pro and in the simulator at three screen widths: small (375 points, like an iPhone mini), medium (393 points) and large (440 points, like a Pro Max). There is no separate iPad version.

### Why is it snowing on the home screen? {#snow}

In December and January it snows on the home screen, in the Southern Hemisphere in June and July.

### Why does the app need no internet and show no ads? {#offline}

Because a calm game doesn't need any of that. All games are created on your iPhone, and the music and images are part of the app. The app makes no internet connections and has no ads, no tracking and no accounts. More on this under [Privacy]({{ '/en/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

### What happens after 14 days? {#after-14-days}

**Planned**, not in the app yet: you download the app for free and can try everything for 14 days. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks it for good. No subscription, no ads. The details will appear here once they are settled.

### I found a bug. {#bug}

Thank you! How to report it is explained under [Support]({{ '/en/support/' | relative_url }}).
